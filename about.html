About the ICT Department

The ICT Department at Aberdare Technical College is committed to providing quality technical and vocational education and training in Information and Communication Technology.

The department equips learners with practical, technical and professional skills required to effectively use, maintain, manage and develop information technology systems.

Training emphasizes practical learning, problem solving, innovation, digital literacy and the application of ICT skills in real-world environments.

Our Vision & Mission

Our Vision

To develop competent, innovative and technology-oriented ICT professionals who can contribute effectively to the digital transformation of society and industry.

Our Mission

To provide quality, practical and industry-oriented ICT training that equips learners with relevant technical, digital, entrepreneurial and professional skills.

Our Values

We promote professionalism, integrity, teamwork, innovation, creativity, discipline, continuous learning and responsible use of technology.
