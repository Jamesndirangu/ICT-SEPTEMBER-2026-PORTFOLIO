Module 3 ICT Units

Unit 1: Install Computer Software

Installation, configuration, updating and maintenance of computer software including operating systems, application software, device drivers and utility programs.

Unit 2: Network Design and Management

Design, configuration, implementation, administration and management of computer networks including network topologies, devices, addressing, protocols, security and troubleshooting.

Unit 3: Basic Electronics

Application of basic electronic principles, electronic components, circuits, measurements and testing techniques used in ICT equipment and electronic systems.

Unit 4: Work Ethics

Application of professional ethics, workplace discipline, teamwork, communication, responsibility, confidentiality, occupational safety and appropriate conduct in the ICT workplace.
